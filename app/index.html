How these deals were checked

Each deal was found in a published Black Friday roundup or a retailer's own sale release dated 17–24 Nov 2026 — Tom's Guide and TechRadar live trackers, 9to5Toys, Best Buy's Black Friday release, Nintendo's announcement, and Product Bible's Amazon price checks — and re-checked against that source on 27 Nov 2026. The card names the source and the date it reported the price. The discount shown is always computed from the two prices on the card, so it adds up. A “was” price is only as honest as the seller, so the verdict line says when a cut is small, when a list price moved recently, or when another store has the same product cheaper — the same product at two stores is shown together so you can compare.

What is not here: no prices guessed from memory, no “up to” sale banners, no carrier bill-credit phone offers (those are monthly credits, not a price cut), and no Apple gift-card offers (you still pay full price). If a price could not be tied to a named source, it was left out.
